# NTG Project Hub v0.10.1 — Material Allowance Schedules

Adds reusable material allowance schedules to contracts, especially useful for T&M and large commercial work.

## Included
- Add/edit/delete allowance items per contract
- Category, description, quantity, unit, allowance amount, actual cost, markup applicability, markup %, and notes
- Allowance total, actual-cost total, and variance shown in the Contract Builder
- Optional contract-level allowance terms
- Customer-facing Material Allowance Schedule on the contract PDF
- Works on commercial, residential, lump-sum, and T&M contracts
