# NTG Project Hub v0.10.9 — Estimated Union Labor Hours Breakdown

Install after v0.10.8.

## Changes
- Customer-facing hybrid/T&M proposal PDF now shows estimated union labor hours by classification.
- Breaks hours into straight time, overtime, double time, and total estimated hours.
- When client billing rates are visible, each classification also shows NTG billing rates and its estimated labor amount.
- When client billing rates are hidden, the proposal still shows estimated hours and the total estimated labor component without disclosing individual rates.
- Estimated Project Cost now includes a clear budget breakdown for estimated union labor, marked-up material allowances, other proposal pricing, and any manual budget adjustment.
- Budgetary language remains clear that final T&M billing is based on actual authorized work and agreed commercial terms.

No Supabase migration is required.
